Best Price Guarantee

Comparable system. Lower installed quote. We'll beat it.

Find a lower quote on comparable equipment and installation and we'll beat it. Best-price customers get priority booking on the schedule.

How it works

Three steps, no haggling.

  1. Get your quote. Any written quote from another company for comparable equipment and installation.
  2. Send it to us. Use the form below or text a photo of it to (952) 952-6206.
  3. We beat it. If it's comparable, we beat the price and move you to priority booking.

What counts as comparable

Same water source (city or well), similar softening capacity and flow rating, similar treatment stages, professional installation included. We'll tell you plainly if a quote isn't comparable and why.
