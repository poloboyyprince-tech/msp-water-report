FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask

How much does a whole-home system cost?
Every price is published on our pricing page. Whole-home city systems run $2,999 to $4,499 and well systems $4,499 to $5,999, with a reverse osmosis drinking-water system included free. Reverse osmosis on its own is $799, a pre-sediment filter is $399 and a UV purifier is $899. Financing options are available, and travel fees may apply beyond 35 miles from Minneapolis.
How long does installation take?
Most installations are completed in a single visit. We connect the equipment to your main water line, configure it for your water and walk you through how it works before we leave.
Should my well water be tested?
Yes, especially if you suspect iron, sulfur or manganese. Treatment depends on the form and concentration of the problem, so we go over testing on the call and set the final configuration from the results.
Can I schedule online?
Yes. Choose a date and time for your phone consultation on our schedule page. No deposit is required, you get a confirmation right away, and we call you at that time.
