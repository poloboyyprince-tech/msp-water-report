City water

Chlorine & Chloramine

Municipal utilities add chlorine or chloramine to keep water safe on its way through the distribution system. It does its job in the pipes, but the taste and smell arrive at your tap along with it.

How MSP approaches it

The Correct Treatment, Not a Generic Box.

The Jacobi catalytic carbon tank is selected specifically because it treats chloramine as well as free chlorine. Every whole-home city system includes it, so showers, laundry and every faucet get treated water.
