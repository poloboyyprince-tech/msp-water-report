Why it matters

The Concerns, Explained

Manganese

The Minnesota Department of Health warns that high manganese can affect memory, attention and motor skills — with infants and children most at risk. It also stains laundry and fixtures brownish-black.

Where it comes from: Naturally occurring in Minnesota groundwater — very common in metro-area wells. · What you notice: Black/brown staining on fixtures and laundry; bitter metallic taste.

Iron

Not a major health risk, but a constant nuisance.

Where it comes from: Naturally dissolved from Minnesota soil and rock into groundwater. · What you notice: Rusty orange/red staining on sinks, tubs, toilets and laundry; metallic taste; clogs fixtures and appliances.
