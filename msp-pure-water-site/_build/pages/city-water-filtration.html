Common City Water Concerns in the Twin Cities

  • Water Hardness: Minnesota groundwater is generally considered hard. Hardness minerals — primarily calcium and magnesium — are responsible for scale buildup on plumbing fixtures, water heaters, and appliances.
  • Chlorine and Chloramine: Municipal water treatment in the Twin Cities uses disinfectants including chloramine. These disinfectants affect taste and odor and require catalytic carbon filtration for effective reduction.
  • Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): Dissolved minerals affect drinking water taste. Point-of-use reverse osmosis addresses TDS concerns at the kitchen faucet.
