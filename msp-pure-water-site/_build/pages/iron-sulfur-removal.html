Well Water Treatment

Iron and Sulfur Removal Systems for Well Water

Iron and hydrogen sulfide are common well water problems in Minnesota. Dissolved iron causes orange or rust-colored staining on fixtures, sinks, and laundry. Hydrogen sulfide produces the characteristic rotten-egg odor. Effective treatment depends on the form of iron and the chemistry of the water.

Dual Tank Well Water Whole Home Filtration System

$4,499

Uses air oxidation — a chemical-free method that creates a controlled air pocket to oxidize dissolved iron and sulfur compounds. Oxidized material is then captured by filtration media and backwashed on a scheduled cycle. Includes softening tank for hardness treatment.

Iron and Sulfur Treatment System

Advanced

$5,999

Uses hydrogen peroxide injection for chemical oxidation — an established treatment method for dissolved iron, manganese, and hydrogen sulfide. The peroxide oxidizes dissolved contaminants into forms that downstream carbon filtration can capture. Includes a dedicated softening tank.

About hydrogen peroxide: The system uses hydrogen peroxide as part of the oxidation process, not as a drinking water additive. Treated water passes through downstream carbon filtration. Proper dosing based on water chemistry is essential and is part of the installation configuration.
