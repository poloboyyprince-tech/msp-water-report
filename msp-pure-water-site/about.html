About Us

Better water for Minnesota homes — honestly assessed, fairly priced, installed cleanly, and backed personally.

Meet the Founder & CEO

Prince

Being from Minnesota himself, Prince understands how important water quality is here. From hard water buildup to the long-term wear it can put on plumbing, fixtures, and appliances, homeowners deserve solutions that help prevent problems before they become expensive repairs.

Prince started this company because he was tired of seeing overpriced water solutions — homeowners quoted thousands more than the work was worth for the same equipment. We're passionate about making water treatment affordable for homeowners, without cutting a single corner on quality.

MSP Pure Water was created to offer Minnesota homeowners a professional, dependable way to protect their health, preserve their plumbing systems, and maintain the value and integrity of their homes through better water quality.

When you work with MSP Pure Water, you work with Prince directly. Every test, every recommendation, and every install runs through him personally. That accountability is not a selling point. It is just how the business operates.
