The MSP Approach

BUILT AROUND THE WATER PROBLEM.
NOT A ONE-SIZE-FITS-ALL BOX.

Traditional Problem

HARD WATER

Scale, spots, buildup on fixtures and appliances.

MSP Solution

High-Capacity Softening Resin

Ion-exchange resin removes calcium and magnesium — the minerals responsible for hard water.

Traditional Problem

CHLORINE / CHLORAMINE

Taste, odor and municipal disinfectants.

MSP Solution

Catalytic Coconut-Shell Carbon

Premium catalytic carbon specifically selected for treating chlorine and chloramine in municipal water.

Traditional Problem

WELL WATER IRON & SULFUR

Staining, metallic taste, rotten-egg odor.

MSP Solution

Air or Chemical Oxidation + Filtration

Oxidation converts dissolved iron and sulfur compounds into forms that downstream filtration can capture.

Traditional Problem

DRINKING WATER

Dissolved contaminants and unwanted taste.

MSP Solution

Reverse Osmosis

A dedicated under-sink RO system provides a separate, highly filtered source for drinking and cooking.
