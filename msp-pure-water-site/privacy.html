Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Information We Collect

When you visit our website or schedule a water filtration installation, we collect certain information about your device, your interaction with the site, and the information necessary to schedule and complete your installation — such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address. We may also collect additional information if you contact us with questions or for support.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and complete your installation or service appointment
  • Communicate with you about appointments, systems, quotes, and promotions
  • Screen requests for potential risk or fraud
  • Improve and optimize our website and services
  • Provide you with information or advertising relating to our systems and services

Sharing Your Information

We share your personal information with service providers to help us provide our services and fulfill our contracts with you. For example, we use payment processors to accept payments and analytics providers to understand site usage. We do not sell your personal information.

Your Privacy Rights

Our services are offered exclusively within the State of Minnesota. As a Minnesota resident, you have rights under the Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act and other applicable US law — including the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request that it be corrected or deleted, and opt out of certain uses of your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email below and we will respond as required by applicable law. We do not sell personal information, and we honor opt-out requests for marketing communications.

SMS & Phone Communications

If you provide your phone number when scheduling or contacting us, we may call or text you about your appointment or inquiry. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Data Retention

When you schedule an installation or service with us, we maintain your appointment and service records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

Contact

For more information about our privacy practices or if you have questions, please contact us by email at info@msppurewater.com.
