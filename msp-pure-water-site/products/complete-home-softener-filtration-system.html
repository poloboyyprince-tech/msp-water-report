Soften and filter every drop of water entering your home with one high-capacity system. The Whole Home Water Filtration and Softening System removes the hardness minerals that cause spots, scale buildup and dry skin, while catalytic carbon strips out chlorine, chloramine, bad taste and odor. Every faucet, shower and appliance in your home gets clean, soft, great-tasting water — and it protects your water heater and appliances for years longer.

  • MSP ProValve: Automatically backwashes and regenerates the system — ceramic discs for longer life, highly configurable, 72-hour memory backup
  • Granular Activated Catalytic Coconut Shell Carbon (15 lbs): Treats chlorine, chloramine, taste and odor
  • USA Made High-Capacity Softening Resin (75 lbs): 10% crosslinked for superior durability — removes hardness with no pressure loss
  • Polishing Gravel: Supports the media bed for even flow and prevents channeling
  • Free Reverse Osmosis Add-On: Your choice of tank or tankless drinking-water system, included with installation
