What It Does

Treats the three biggest well-water problems — iron, sulfur odor and hardness — chemical-free. Air-oxidation technology plus Katalox Light media handle staining and rotten-egg smell while a dedicated resin tank softens your water.

How It Works

  1. 1The MSP AIO ProValve creates a pressurized air pocket that naturally oxidizes iron, sulfur and manganese — no chemicals added.
  2. 2Katalox Light media traps the oxidized particles and boosts pH for better-tasting water.
  3. 3A dedicated resin tank removes calcium and magnesium hardness.
  4. 4Both tanks self-clean with automatic backwashing.
